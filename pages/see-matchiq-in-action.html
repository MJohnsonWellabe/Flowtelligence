What the Demo Shows

From a Friendly Chat to the Exact Right Coverage

It Listens First

Every question opens by acknowledging what you just said. MatchIQ reacts to your actual answers — a cancer concern, a dental worry, a tight budget — so the conversation feels like talking to a knowledgeable advisor, not filling out a form.

Rider-Level Matching

MatchIQ doesn't just name a product. It pinpoints the specific components you need — an ER rider on Hospital Indemnity, a Heart Attack & Stroke rider on Cancer coverage, the right plan level on Medicare Supplement — based on what matters most to you.

Fifty-Plus Tailored Paths

Concern, sub-need, age, health, tobacco, enrollment, and budget combine into dozens of distinct recommendation paths. Two people rarely land in the same place — because their situations aren't the same.

A Confident Recommendation

The conversation ends the way a great agent would: a warm, specific endorsement of the coverage that fits, with the reasoning laid out and a clear next step to apply.
